# Credits

The Stormlight Archive and its characters belong to Brandon Sanderson / Dragonsteel Entertainment.
The artworks below belong to their respective artists. They are used here, cropped and resized,
in a private, unlisted, non-commercial fan project. Nothing here is offered for sale and no
ownership is claimed. Credits never appear on the cards themselves.

## Substitutions

- **Thude -> Rlain.** The Coppermind has no usable art of Thude, so the Singers' second Archer
  is **Rlain** (a Listener) instead, with character id `rlain` (card `black-archer-rlain`),
  using his portrait by Marie Seeberger.
